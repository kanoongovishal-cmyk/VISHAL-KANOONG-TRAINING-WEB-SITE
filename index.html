Which certification should I choose?

Start with the work you do and the experience you can demonstrate. PMP focuses on project management; PgMP on managing related projects for benefits; PfMP on portfolio decisions aligned with strategy. Check the certification body's current eligibility requirements before applying.

Can I learn online or in the UAE?

Enquire about live online and face-to-face options. Dates, location and delivery format are confirmed for each programme.

What is included in the course fee?

Request a written proposal confirming teaching hours, materials, practice questions and any student kit. Confirm separately whether an examination fee is included.

Does completing training award the certification?

Training supports preparation. Professional certification is awarded by the relevant certification body after its eligibility, application and examination requirements are met.

Can you provide a private corporate batch?

Yes, enquire with your preferred programme, audience size, location and dates to discuss a proposal.
